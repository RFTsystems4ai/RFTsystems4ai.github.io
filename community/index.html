COMMUNITY STANDARD

Claims are stronger when other people can test them.

No receipt → no claim.

When discussing a demonstration result, include enough public-safe information for another person to reproduce or independently inspect it.

A verified artefact establishes only the verification scope it declares. It does not make an underlying factual statement automatically true.

COMMUNITY RULES

Challenge the system, not each other.

Evidence first

Separate what you observed from what you infer.

Failures stay useful

A legitimate FAIL is evidence, not something to hide.

Protect private information

Do not post credentials, signing keys, private company data or confidential material.

Keep claims scoped

Describe exactly what a test establishes and avoid stronger claims than the evidence supports.

Responsible testing

Test RFTSystems4Ai demonstrations and material you are authorised to test. Do not target third parties.

Professional discussion

Hard technical criticism is welcome. Personal attacks are not.
